## What changed

react-dockable-desktop 7.4.0's fixes from a consumer's field report, ported ([0019](../decisions/0019-frost-on-a-pseudo-element.md)).
The stylesheet change is one transform, proven on rdd first (run on rdd 7.3.0 it reproduces rdd
7.4.0 byte for byte):

- **Frost on `::before`.** This covers the floating window, the drawer, the overlay widget, the frosted panel toolbar and the macOS docked panel.
  - Where the frost saturates, the background moves with it.
  - The pseudo-element extends 1px under a border, except where the container clips its overflow.
- **Reduced motion.** It works as `:animations="false"` does.
- **`<VddSidebar>` and `<VddToasts>` follow `setDirection('rtl')`**, and so does everything inside the sidebar. That scope was the owner's decision.
- **`Label` gains `() => string`.** `saveLayout` leaves a function title out, and a restored panel takes its registered default.
- **Non-finite saved geometry is repaired** in `core/serialize.ts`.
- **Docs:**
  - the reactivity notes (panels chapter), frost and motion (theming chapter), and title functions and direction (i18n chapter);
  - CHANGELOG 1.5.0, ADR 0019, PARITY.md and the plan's M17. Version 1.5.0.
